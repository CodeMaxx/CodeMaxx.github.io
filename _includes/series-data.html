{%- comment -%} Collects everything known about one series. Arguments: id: the series' file name in _series/ (the `series` value of its posts). Sets, in the caller's scope: series_doc the _series document, or nil when the id is unknown series_posts the published posts in the series series_keys every part in reading order, each "||". Kind `post` indexes series_posts; kind `plan` indexes series_doc.planned. A planned part is left out once a post carries the same part number. series_count how many parts there are, published and planned series_unit what a part is called ("Part" unless the series says so) series_units the same in lower case, plural unless there is one part series_show_total false when the series opts out of "of N" {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_doc = site.series | where: 'slug', include.id | first -%} {%- assign series_posts = '' | split: '' -%} {%- assign series_keys = '' | split: '' -%} {%- assign series_unit = 'Part' -%} {%- assign series_show_total = true -%} {%- if series_doc -%} {%- assign series_posts = site.posts | where: 'series', include.id -%} {%- assign series_unit = series_doc.unit | default: 'Part' -%} {%- if series_doc.show_total == false -%} {%- assign series_show_total = false -%} {%- endif -%} {%- comment -%} Adding 100000 pads the part number so the keys sort as text. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_taken = '|' -%} {%- for series_item in series_posts -%} {%- capture series_key -%} {{- series_item.series_part | plus: 100000 }}|post|{{ forloop.index0 -}} {%- endcapture -%} {%- assign series_keys = series_keys | push: series_key -%} {%- assign series_taken = series_taken | append: series_item.series_part | append: '|' -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- for series_item in series_doc.planned -%} {%- capture series_key -%}|{{ series_item.part }}|{%- endcapture -%} {%- unless series_taken contains series_key -%} {%- capture series_key -%} {{- series_item.part | plus: 100000 }}|plan|{{ forloop.index0 -}} {%- endcapture -%} {%- assign series_keys = series_keys | push: series_key -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign series_keys = series_keys | sort -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign series_count = series_keys | size -%} {%- assign series_units = series_unit | downcase -%} {%- unless series_count == 1 -%} {%- assign series_units = series_units | append: 's' -%} {%- endunless -%}
